# Contributing to DuckLake

## Writing Hours

The initial development of Contract Checker is being done as a CTO office innovation project within Xebia Data's Analytics Engineering Cell. As such, everyone (including people outside this group) are more than welcome to contribute to Ducklake. Currently, the core team of Ducklake consists of the following people:

- Thom van Engelenburg
- Sonu Jacob

These 2 people will be using CTO office hours to develop Contract Checker initially.

If you are not one of these people but you want to help out, that's great! You're free to join any our XKE's and XD Fridays. If you do spend time on the Contract Checker during XD Friday, please use the 'WBSO: Innovatie AE cell' hour code in X3 and put "XD Friday Contract Checker" in the description. This way, we can attribute your work to the project in the right way and ensure the finance team can process the hours correctly.
